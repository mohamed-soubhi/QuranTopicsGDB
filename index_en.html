Digitization & Canonical Verification

The printed index was extracted and verified through advanced multi-stage processing:

  • Two-Column Isolation: Following Arabic reading order (Right Column top-to-bottom, then Left Column) preventing cross-column text collision.
  • Color Channel Separation: Distinguishing red Western Surah digits from black Eastern Ayah ranges.
  • Canonical Hafs Bounds: Validating every citation against canonical Hafs 'an 'Asim bounds (Kufi 6,236 Ayahs).
  • Marqoom Standards: Strict adherence to standardized fields (surah_number, ayah_number).

Tri-Model Audit & Print Discovery

Cross-evaluation across three independent models (Claude vs Gemini vs DeepSeek) achieved maximum confidence:

  • Claude Superiority: Achieved 99.992% accuracy with robust bracket encapsulation.
  • Authentic Source Misprint: Detected an authentic typographical error in the source book on Page 25 of the PDF (which corresponds to printed page 24 in the book and HTML pages) under topic "أهواله" (Surah 77:78, where Surah Al-Mursalat only has 50 ayahs). Documented faithfully in metadata.
  • Golden Dataset: Unified all verified records into data/quran_topics.json.
