2. Typographic & Structural Breakdown

A. Two-Column Layout (Right-to-Left Classical Reading Order)

Arabic book indices are formatted into two columns read Right Column first, then Left Column.

  • Claude: Perfectly tracked columns. Each block contains an explicit column: "right" or column: "left" property, preserving exact visual coordinates.
  • Gemini: Performed line scans across the page. On dense pages (such as Page 2), lines from the right column (إرادته) blended horizontally into the left column (عقائد البشر وأهواؤهم), merging two distinct topics.
  • Ollama (Ours): Pre-split the page image into two distinct vertical images before LLM inference, ensuring clean column isolation. However, y-axis vertical chunking split some lengthy topics into multiple pieces.

B. Distinguishing Red Surah Numbers from Black Ayah Numbers

In the source book, Surah numbers are printed in red ink, while Ayah numbers and ranges are printed in black ink. When numbers wrap across lines (e.g. line ending with red 5 and the next line starting with ayahs ٦ و١٨ و٥٢), models must attribute the black ayahs to Surah 5.

  • Claude: Wrapped each detected red Surah number in curly brackets ({surah}), maintaining continuity even across complex line breaks.
  • Ollama (Ours): Color masking successfully separated red from black pixels, but OCR occasionally concatenated line-wrapped numbers (e.g. reading ayahs 10 and 14 as 109 for Surah 44).

3. Authentic Physical Print Errors Discovered

Confirmed Source Misprint (Page 25):

Under the topic أهواله on Page 25, Claude and our validator both flagged a citation: Surah 77, Ayah 78. Canonical Quranic rules establish that Surah Al-Mursalat (77) has only 50 ayahs.

Direct visual inspection of the original scanned PDF page confirmed that the printed book itself contains this typographical error ('78')! Claude noted this anomaly explicitly as confirmed_as_printed. This single discrepancy accounts for the 0.008% difference in Claude's score.

Confirmed Out-of-Order Ayahs (Page 47):

Under topic يد on Page 47, Surah 12 ayahs were typeset out of numerical order in the source book (ayah 50 printed before ayah 31).

4. Golden Dataset Synthesis & Marqoom Compliance

Why the Golden Dataset achieves Maximum Confidence:

  • Standardized Terminology: Converted all internal identifiers to surah_number, ayah_number, and ayahs.

  • Pre-Commit Enforcement: Both quranic-data and quranic-terminology hooks run on every commit and pass with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
